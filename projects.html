Projects

Here are some of the projects I have worked on.

Hey, Hobby

Hey, Hobby is an app my robotics team and I created to help solve one problem. That problem was that not many people connect with their friends and each other's hobbies, and we solved that problem by creating Hey, Hobby. You can learn more by visiting the FLL page Hey Hobby demo.

Robot Car

I created a robot car which can be controlled using a joystick. The controller connects to the car using two ESP32 modules wirelessly. The car can move in all directions using the joystick.
