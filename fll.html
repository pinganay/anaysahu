First Lego League

First Lego Leauge, also known as FLL is a competition where you compete with other teams to create a robot that solves missions on a game board. Your team also has to come up with a problem that needs to be solved come come up with a solution that fits the theme of the competition. For example, in the 2022 season, the theme was "SuperPowered", where the game board had differnet energy sources we had to deliver to different areas, and where we had to research a problem with energy and come up with a solution. I have been in FLL for 4 years and it has been an amazing experience.
