First Lego League

First Lego Leauge, also known as FLL is a competition where you compete with other teams to create a robot that solves missions on a game board. Your team also has to come up with a problem that needs to be solved come come up with a solution that fits the theme of the competition. For example, in the 2022 season, the theme was "SuperPowered", where the game board had different energy sources we had to deliver to different areas, and where we had to research a problem with energy and come up with a solution. I have been in FLL for 4 years and it has been an amazing experience.

Year 1: 2022-2023

I joined in the middle of the year and was able to learn the basics of robotics and teamwork. I got used to the different pieces and how they connect together, and the coding environment. We used block coding, which was a great way to get started with programming. The theme for this year was "SuperPowered", which was about different energy sources and their applications. We figured out that most our sources of electricity rely on inconsistent sources of energy. So me and my team worked together to create a innovation solution. Our solution was to generate more energy when the source is available and store that energy for when the demand is greater than what the non-consistent sources can provide. Year 2: 2023-2024

In my second year, I improved my skills in programming and design. We worked on a new theme for the competition and created a better robot by looking at the problems with our old one and solving them. This years theme was "Masterpiece" which was about different forms of art and technology. We looked at different problems related to this theme, and figured out that a lot of people weren’t aware of the hobbies that everyone liked to do, and most of us didn’t use social media platforms, making it harder to share our interests with other people. So we created a solution called "Hey, Hobby" which is an app that connects people with similar hobbies and interests (A video is shown below). We also made a huge improvement to our robot, turning it from a differential robot to a swerve robot. This allowed us to move in any direction without having to turn the robot, which made it much faster and more efficient. We also improved our coding skills by learning how to use PID control, which allowed us to make our robot more accurate and consistent. A picture of our robot is shown below.

We made it to regionals again!
