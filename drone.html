My Drone Project

I’m building my own drone to learn more about flight, electronics, and robotics. This project is a fun way for me to combine engineering, creativity, and hands-on problem solving. I will be updating this page as I make progress on the project.

Day 1 - July 11, 2026

Today I started my drone project! I’m excited to learn more about flight, electronics, and robotics. I plan to build a drone from scratch, which will be a fun challenge and a good way to spend the summer. I got most of the necessary parts today, and I already started building the frame and attaching the motors onto the frame.

Day 2 - July 12, 2026

Today, I realized that the bullet connectors on my motor have nothing to connect to on the ESC. I need to buy the connectors and some wire to solder onto the ESC. I also attached the propellers onto the motors to see how it would look, although I have to take them off later while calibrating the flight controller and the ESC. The connectors will come tomorrow, so I will be able to complete the assembly of the motors.

Day 3 - July 13, 2026

When the wires and bullet connectors came today, I was super excited to solder them onto the ESC. But the connectors were not connecting onto the wires, and it took up the whole day trying to figure out why. I tried figuring out the issue, and I thought that the soldering iron wasn't getting hot enough, which caused the solder to not melt inside of the cavity in the bullet connectors. But anyways, that didn't work, so I ordered some pre-assembled bullet connectors.

Day 4 - July 14, 2026

Today, I figured that I could solder on the receiver to the flight controller since the wires were coming tomorrow and the day after. So I took the receiver and connected some wires onto it, and then started soldering the other ends onto the flight controller. It took a while, even for 3 wires, and I think it is because I am still learning how to actually solder properly. But the wires are soldered on, so that's one thing completed for the flight controller. I will wait for the wires to come tomorrow.

Day 5 - July 15, 2026

Today, the wires arrived! I was so excited to finally start assembling the ESC. But there was one problem. The soldering iron wasn't getting hot enough, which caused the solder to not melt properly onto the wires and the pads on the ESC. This was mainly because the soldering iron tip was completley oxidised, and I had to clean it. But the oxidisation was worse than I thought, and I couldn't use it. I thought maybe getting a new soldering iron would solve the problem. So i placed an order for a new one. It will come tommorow.

Day 6 - July 16, 2026

I took the new soldering iron and started assembling the ESC. This new soldering kit came with a lot of things, like a heat gun, desoldering pump, and some tweezers. Before it came though, I watched countless videos on how to solder and maintain the health of iron and the tips. But the oxidised tip was still a problem, and I had to replace it. I used a new tip that came with the new soldering kit, and it still didn't work. I was super frustrated, and decided to work on it tommorow, and clean the tip with some brass wool.

Day 7 - July 17, 2026

I couldn't get the soldering to work properly, and I was getting really frustrated. I decided to take a break for a while as I also have to focus on my high school work, as I am about to start my freshman year.
